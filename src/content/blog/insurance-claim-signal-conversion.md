## Raw signals are not the win
A weather alert, incident report, or permit record only matters if your team can tell whether it is fresh, relevant, reachable, and worth acting on.

## A conversion-ready workflow looks like this
1. Detect the signal and preserve source provenance
2. Enrich with address, service type, and contact context
3. Suppress duplicates and weak matches
4. Route to the best-fit territory
5. Recommend the next action clearly
6. Convert the approved opportunity into a lead and booked job

## Why signal-heavy teams still underperform
They confuse activity with quality. Operators get flooded with cards that all look equally urgent, so the queue becomes background noise.

## What improves close rate
- fresher signals rise above stale ones
- local incidents outrank remote ones
- duplicates reuse the same lead identity
- review-required opportunities are clearly gated
- conversion into a job is one short path, not a scavenger hunt

## The practical takeaway
Signal conversion is a discipline. Better routing, explainability, and duplicate control matter more than simply adding another source.
